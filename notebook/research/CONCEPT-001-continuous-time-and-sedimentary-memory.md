# Continuous time and sedimentary memory

Strata combines two models that should not be collapsed:

- a chronological ledger that can answer exact interval and reporting questions;
- a sedimentary artifact that embodies accumulated time through mass, color, topology, and broad chronology.

Idle is not an absence of time. It is the baseline state of continuous deposition outside ordinary active-time accounting. Selecting a layer changes classification, not whether time exists.

The sediment is neither a canonical accounting database nor disposable decoration. It is an accountable artistic projection: exact mass and per-layer contribution require conservation, while organic topology may evolve only through declared simulation rules rather than administrative accidents such as resize or preview rendering.

This synthesis supports current accepted doctrine but leaves vertical chronology, layer/context structure, formation lifecycle, balance terminology, and configurable quantum open.
