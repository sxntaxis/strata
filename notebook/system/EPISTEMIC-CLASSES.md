# Epistemic classes

Strata combines measurable timekeeping, simulation behavior, personal meaning, and artistic intent. These require explicit claim classes.

## Classes

### Observed

Directly present in source, tests, persisted data, command output, or controlled runtime observation.

Example: the current implementation creates one attempted grain per spawn tick.

### Reported

Stated by the product owner or another person but not independently observed in the current environment.

Example: losing sediment is emotionally damaging even when ledger totals remain available.

Owner reports may establish product intent without becoming runtime evidence.

### Derived

Calculated or logically entailed from explicit inputs.

Example: at one grain per second, a one-hour active interval owes 3,600 grains.

### Interpretation

A reasoned explanation of observed or reported material.

Example: continuous idle deposition expresses that time continues even when the user is not actively classifying it.

### Assumption

A temporary belief required to proceed but not yet supported strongly enough.

Example: users will prefer automatic previous-layer continuation after an unexpected crash.

### Hypothesis

A testable proposed explanation or product direction.

Example: broad chronology with local physical emergence will preserve both the geological metaphor and living sand behavior.

### Intention

A desired direction that has not passed its decision gate.

Example: temporal quantum may become configurable later.

### Decision

An explicitly accepted, rejected, deferred, or superseded choice with an owner and consequence.

### Unknown

A material question whose answer could change behavior, architecture, migration, or interpretation.

## Rules

- Polished prose does not upgrade a claim's class.
- Source presence does not establish intended product behavior.
- Owner intent does not establish current implementation behavior.
- A raw outsider reaction is perception evidence, not product authority.
- An issue proposal is not an accepted solution.
- Artistic meaning is a legitimate product requirement when explicitly accepted.
- Uncertainty must remain visible when designing schemas or migrations.
